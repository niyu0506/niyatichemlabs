Certification is the result of a routine, not a substitute for one. Every consignment of raw
material is analysed on arrival — identity, purity and, where relevant, assay of the marker
compound — before it is accepted into stores. Material that fails specification is rejected
rather than reworked.

Testing is done in our own laboratory on site, so results come back in hours rather than
weeks and a doubtful batch can be held before it reaches production. In-process checks cover
blend uniformity, weight, hardness and disintegration as appropriate to the dosage form.
Finished batches are tested again and released only against a signed specification, and
retained samples are held so that a question raised later can be answered against the
material actually shipped.

## Documentation you can ask for

A certificate of analysis accompanies every consignment as a matter of course. Beyond that we
can supply batch manufacturing records, stability data, heavy-metal and microbial test
reports, and the export paperwork your market requires. If your quality team or your
regulator needs a particular document, ask for it — in most cases it is already on file.
