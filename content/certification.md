## Storage and handling

Materials are held and dispatched in their original sealed packing. Our standing advice, which
matches what is printed on the pack, is to store in a clean, dry, infestation-free area away
from direct sunlight, and to observe the shelf life stated on the batch documentation.

## Ask for anything you need

If your QA/QC team or your auditor needs a particular document, ask for it. If we hold it we
will send it; if we do not, we will tell you where it has to come from.
