## About this formulation

Acai (*Euterpe oleracea*) is the fruit of a slender palm indigenous to the floodplains and
rain forests of the Amazon basin, where the berry has long been part of the daily diet and is
valued as a restorative food. It is an exotic relative of the blueberry and the cranberry, and
like them it owes much of its character to the deep pigments concentrated in the skin and pulp.

Acai Berry Capsule presents that fruit as a standardised 10:1 dry extract, so that a single
400 mg capsule carries the constituents of a far larger quantity of fresh berry in a form that
is stable, uniform and convenient to dose.

## The extract, and what it carries

**Anthocyanins and polyphenols** — the pigments that give the ripe berry its colour are also
its principal antioxidant constituents, and they are the reason the fruit is concentrated
rather than simply dried.

**Vitamin C** — present in the extract at 5%, contributing to antioxidant activity and to the
normal function of the immune system and of collagen formation in skin.

**B-group vitamins, electrolytes and trace minerals** — the fruit naturally supplies a range
of micronutrients associated with normal energy metabolism, and these are carried through into
the extract.

**Antioxidant capacity** — acai is frequently cited for its ORAC (Oxygen Radical Absorbance
Capacity) value, a laboratory measure used to compare the antioxidant capacity of foods. It is
one of the reasons the berry is used in formulations aimed at oxidative stress rather than at
any single organ system.
