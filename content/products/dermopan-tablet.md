## About this formulation

In ayurveda, skin complaints of the dermatitis type are read as a *pitta* problem with a
digestive origin. An unsuitable diet and irregular routine disturb digestion, *pitta* — the fire
principle — is aggravated, and it expresses itself through the skin as heat, while toxins settle
into the deeper tissues. Management therefore works from the inside out, on the blood and the
gut, rather than on the skin alone. Dermopan Tablet is a 500 mg film-coated tablet that
combines bitter blood-purifying herbs with two classical mineral preparations along those
lines.

## The herbs, and why they are here

**Shuddha Gandhak (purified sulphur)** — the largest single ingredient. Sulphur has a long
record in ayurvedic dermatology, where it is credited with blood-purifying action and with
helping the body clear accumulated toxins.

**Kutki (Picrorhiza kurroa)** — a bitter used to support the liver, the blood and the
gastrointestinal tract. It is traditionally associated with a clearer complexion and with
easing skin inflammation, itching and burning.

**Amla (Emblica officinalis)** — a *rasayana* that nourishes the tissues and is understood in
ayurveda to relieve excess internal heat, which is why it appears so often in skin
formulations.

**Neem (Azadirachta indica)** — the classical blood purifier of Indian medicine, used to clear
toxins from the system and to settle the itchy rashes that accompany dermatitis.

**Chirata (Swertia chirata)** — a bitter used traditionally to settle inflammation, normalise
appetite and support comfortable digestion, which is the digestive half of the picture. It is
also anthelmintic and antimicrobial, and is described in classical texts as a skin-friendly
herb.

**Vavding (Embelia ribes)** — used traditionally for worm infestation, and for its antifungal
and antibacterial character in a range of skin disorders.

**Pitpapado (Fumaria vaillantii)** — a blood purifier used traditionally for boils, eczema and
psoriasis-type presentations, as well as for acne and constipation.

**Ras Manikya** — a classical mineral preparation used in ring worm, dry or weeping eczema,
scabies, psoriasis and urticaria.

**Praval Pishti** — coral calcium, included as a cooling ingredient for burning sensations,
acidity and calcium deficiency.
