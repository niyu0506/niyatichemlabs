## About this formulation

Ayurvedic literature regards periodic internal cleansing as part of ordinary upkeep rather than
as a response to illness, and the herbs used for it have been in continuous household use for
centuries. The reasoning is plain enough — incomplete digestion leaves waste behind, and waste
is easier to clear before it settles. Detox Tablet is a coated 500 mg tablet combining five
ingredients selected for that purpose, weighted toward the lower bowel and taken at night so it
works while the body is at rest.

## The herbs, and why they are here

**Sonamukhi (Cassia angustifolia)** — senna, and the largest ingredient at 150 mg. Its action is
chiefly on the lower bowel, where it increases the peristaltic movement of the colon by acting
locally on the intestinal wall. It is traditionally considered most suitable where constipation
has become habitual.

**Harde (Terminalia chebula)** — haritaki, described in the classical texts as laxative,
purgative, astringent and rejuvenative at once. It improves the working of the digestive system
and clears unnecessary waste, and is also used as a nervine tonic.

**Trikatu** — the classical combination of three warming herbs, *Zingiber officinale*, *Piper
longum* and *Piper nigrum*. It is a digestive aid in its own right, useful where there is loss
of appetite, indigestion, belching, nausea or flatulence, and it improves the penetration of the
other ingredients into the system.

**Bilvagarbha (Aegle marmelos)** — bael fruit pulp, used across the Ayurvedic, Unani and Siddha
systems in India, Bangladesh and Sri Lanka. Here it balances the purgative herbs, being
traditionally used to settle the intestinal lining and firm the stool.

**Nishottar (Operculina turpethum)** — trivrit. The resin fraction acts as a mild purgative,
which is why only 50 mg is needed, and the herb is also described in traditional use as
anthelmintic and anti-inflammatory.
