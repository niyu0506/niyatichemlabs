## About this formulation

Garcipan Tablet is a single-herb preparation built around the dried fruit rind of *Garcinia
gummi-gutta*, known in ayurveda as Vrikshamla and in the trade as Garcinia cambogia. Each
coated tablet carries 1000 mg of extract standardised to 60 per cent hydroxycitric acid (HCA),
the constituent the formulation is designed around. It is intended as a supporting product
within a weight-management routine that also includes a controlled diet and regular activity,
rather than as a substitute for either.

## The herb, and why it is here

**Vrikshamla (Garcinia gummi-gutta)** — the sour rind of the fruit has long been used along
the western coast of India both as a souring agent in cooking and, in traditional practice, as
a digestive drug. Present-day interest centres on hydroxycitric acid, drawn from the peel,
which is associated with appetite regulation through serotonin uptake in the brain and with
the enzyme pathway involved in converting surplus carbohydrate into stored fat. The same
constituent has been examined in relation to cholesterol and triglyceride levels in overweight
adults.

Standardising the extract to a declared 60 per cent HCA means each batch delivers a stated
quantity of the marker compound rather than a variable one. That matters for the consistency
of the finished tablet and for the specification your own quality team will hold us to.

Because the formulation is single-herb, the dosage form carries the rest of the work. The
tablet is film-coated with HPMC, which keeps the characteristically sour extract palatable and
protects the core from moisture during storage and transport in Indian conditions.
