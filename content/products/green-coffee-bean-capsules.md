## About this formulation

Green coffee beans are simply coffee beans that have not been roasted. The distinction matters
because roasting, which develops the aroma and flavour of the beverage, also reduces the
chlorogenic acid content of the bean. Unroasted beans therefore carry a markedly higher level
of chlorogenic acids than the roasted coffee most people drink, and it is that fraction, not
the caffeine, that the extract is made for.

Green Coffee Bean Capsule contains 400 mg of dry extract per capsule, assayed to not less than
50% chlorogenic acids.

## The extract, and what it carries

**Chlorogenic acids** — the constituent by which the extract is standardised. They are the
compounds associated with green coffee's use in weight management, in supporting glucose
metabolism and in cardiovascular formulations, and they are the reason the bean is processed
green rather than roasted.

**Antioxidant polyphenols** — beyond the chlorogenic acid fraction, the unroasted bean carries
a broad group of polyphenols that contribute to the extract's overall antioxidant capacity.

**Naturally occurring caffeine** — present in the bean and therefore in the extract, though at
a level determined by the raw material rather than added. It is worth noting on label copy for
markets that require a caffeine declaration.

Because the formulation is single-ingredient and standardised, it suits customers building a
slimming, sports nutrition or metabolic support range, and it blends readily with other actives
where a combination product is wanted.
