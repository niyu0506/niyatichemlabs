## About this formulation

Moringano F Tablet is built on Shigru (Moringa oleifera), which makes up 200 mg of the
500 mg coated tablet, combined with the group of herbs that ayurveda has traditionally
reserved for women's health — Shatavari, Ashok, Lodhra and Dashmool. The intention of the
formulation is nutritional and supportive rather than corrective: a daily tablet that sits
alongside ordinary diet and routine.

## The herbs, and why they are here

**Shigru (Moringa oleifera)** — the drumstick tree, valued in Indian households as a dense
dietary source of vitamins, minerals and antioxidant compounds. It forms the nutritional base
of the tablet.

**Shatavari (Asparagus racemosus)** — the classical *rasayana* for women, whose name is
usually translated as "she who has a hundred roots". It is used across the reproductive years
and is traditionally associated with hormonal balance.

**Ashok (Saraca asoca)** — the bark is the best known ayurvedic herb for the menstrual cycle,
used in Indian practice to support normal flow and cycle regularity.

**Lodhra (Symplocos racemosa)** — bark extract, traditionally paired with Ashok in
formulations intended to support uterine tone.

**Dashmool** — the classical ten-root preparation, used in ayurveda where *vata* is aggravated
and associated with cramping and discomfort.

**Aloe (Aloe vera)** — known in ayurveda as Kumari, used both for digestion and as a supportive
herb in women's formulations.

**Methi (Trigonella foenum-graecum)** — fenugreek, rich in dietary fibre and long established
in ayurvedic practice as an anti-inflammatory herb.
