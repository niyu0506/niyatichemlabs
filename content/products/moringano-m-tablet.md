## About this formulation

Moringano M Tablet pairs a substantial dose of Shigru (Moringa oleifera) with four herbs that
ayurveda has traditionally used to support male vitality and resilience. Moringa accounts for
300 mg of the 550 mg coated tablet and provides the nutritional base; Ashwagandha, Shilajit,
Safed Musli and Kaunch Beej are added at supportive levels. The formulation is intended as a
daily tonic taken alongside a normal diet.

## The herbs, and why they are here

**Shigru (Moringa oleifera)** — the drumstick tree, one of the most nutrient-dense plants in
common Indian use. The leaf is a dietary source of vitamins, minerals and antioxidant
compounds, and is traditionally used as a general restorative.

**Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera)** — the best studied ayurvedic adaptogen, sometimes called
Indian ginseng. It is traditionally used to help the body cope with physical and mental stress,
to support endurance, and to promote a calm and steady state of mind.

**Shuddha Shilajit (Asphaltum)** — purified mineral pitch, described in ayurveda as enhancing
the action of the herbs it accompanies by supporting their absorption. It is traditionally
associated with stamina, recovery and antioxidant support.

**Safed Musli (Asparagus adscendens)** — the tuberous root has been used for centuries in
Indian practice as a *vajikarana* herb, that is, one traditionally associated with male
reproductive health and vigour.

**Kaunch Beej (Mucuna pruriens)** — the seed is a classical nervine tonic in ayurveda,
traditionally used to support the normal functioning of the nervous system, mental alertness
and muscular strength.
