## About this formulation

The papaya tree is grown across India for its fruit, but it is the leaf that carries the
highest concentration of proteolytic enzymes, and it is the leaf that Ayurvedic and folk
practice has used for digestion and during convalescence. Papaya Leaf Capsule is a dry extract
of *Carica papaya* leaf, 400 mg per capsule, assayed to not less than 10% papain.

Demand for this product in India is seasonal and driven largely by its traditional use during
the monsoon months, so we plan raw material procurement and batch scheduling accordingly.

## The leaf, and what it carries

**Papain and chymopapain** — the two principal proteolytic enzymes of the leaf. They are
associated with the digestion of protein, with cleansing of the colon, with regular bowel
movement and with a settled stomach lining.

**Amylase and protease** — a further group of enzymes present in the leaf, which act on
carbohydrates, proteins and minerals. Their contribution is less to digestion for its own sake
than to how much nutrition the body draws from a given meal.

**Antioxidants and micronutrients** — the leaf supplies a range of antioxidant compounds and
nutrients associated with supporting the immune system, and with the eye and joint health
claims made for papaya in traditional practice.

**Traditional platelet support** — papaya leaf is widely used in India during and after
febrile illness for its association with the regeneration of platelets and white blood cells.
This is the use for which the leaf is best known domestically.
