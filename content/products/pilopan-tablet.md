## About this formulation

In ayurveda, piles are described as *Arsha* — a condition in which vitiated *apana vata* and a
weakened digestive fire together produce hard stool, straining and swelling of the veins around
the anal canal. Management is therefore approached from two directions at once: keeping the
bowel soft and regular, and calming the local tissue. Pilopan Tablet brings seven traditional
ingredients together on that pattern, combining gentle bowel-regulating herbs, bitter digestives
and cooling astringents in a single 500 mg tablet.

## The herbs, and why they are here

**Haritaki (Terminalia chebula)** — one of the three fruits of *Triphala*, and the herb most
often used in ayurveda to keep the bowel moving without harshness. It is also carminative and is
traditionally associated with tissue repair and healthy digestion.

**Indrajav (Holarrhena antidysenterica)** — the seed is a classical ayurvedic material for the
lower digestive tract, traditionally used to support regular, comfortable bowel function.

**Daru Haridra (Berberis aristata)** — a bitter, berberine-bearing bark used to support
digestion, liver function and normal metabolism, with traditional antibacterial and
anti-inflammatory associations.

**Haridra (Curcuma longa)** — turmeric, whose curcuminoids are among the best-studied plant
antioxidant and anti-inflammatory compounds, included here for tissue comfort.

**Aloe vera (Aloe barbadensis)** — cooling and astringent, traditionally used to settle
irritation, itching and a burning sensation in the ano-rectal area.

**Shuddha Guggul (Commiphora mukul)** — purified guggul resin, used in ayurveda where swelling,
discomfort and restricted movement are present.

**Shuddha Sphatik (Alumen)** — purified alum, a classical mineral astringent long used to
support normal blood flow and tone in the affected tissue.
