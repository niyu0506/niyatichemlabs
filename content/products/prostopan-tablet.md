## About this formulation

Prostopan Tablet is a men's urinary and prostate formulation in which the herbs are processed
in *Kulath kwath* — a decoction of horse gram — before tabletting, following the classical
ayurvedic practice of using a herbal decoction as the processing medium rather than plain
water. Pashanbhed accounts for 200 mg of the 550 mg tablet, with Gokshur, Varun and Punarnava
forming the supporting urinary group.

## The herbs, and why they are here

**Pashanbhed (Bergenia ligulata)** — the name translates as "stone breaker", and the herb is
the classical ayurvedic *lithotriptic*. Its diuretic and cooling action is traditionally used
where urination is frequent, urgent or uncomfortable.

**Gokshur (Tribulus terrestris)** — regarded in ayurveda as among the best herbs for the
kidneys. It is traditionally used to support the flow of urine and to soothe the membranes of
the urinary tract.

**Varun (Crataeva nurvala)** — the traditional ayurvedic herb for the kidneys and prostate,
long used where swelling of the gland makes urination difficult.

**Punarnava (Boerhaavia diffusa)** — the aerial parts are diuretic and antispasmodic in
ayurvedic use, and are traditionally applied in urinary tract discomfort.

**Kanchnar (Bauhinia variegata)** — the classical herb for glandular swellings. Its
constituents are traditionally described as clearing impurities from fat, blood and muscle
tissue so that the gland can function normally.

**Shuddha Guggul (Commiphora mukul)** — purified gum resin, included for its traditional role
in glandular and inflammatory conditions.

**Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera)** — an adaptogen that supports the ability to cope with
stress, and is traditionally described as nourishing muscle and bone tissue and calming *vata*.

**Kulath (Macrotyloma uniflorum)** — horse gram, used here as the processing decoction. It is
astringent, diuretic and tonic, and is classically associated with the breaking down and
flushing out of urinary deposits.
