## About this formulation

Sustained stress rarely presents as one symptom. It shows up as disturbed sleep, loss of mental
concentration, irritability and a general sense of being wound up, and over time it draws in the
digestion and the circulation as well. Ayurveda reads this through three sub-doshas that govern
the mind: *prana vata*, which governs the brain and sensory perception, *tarpaka kapha*, which
governs the cerebro-spinal fluid, and *sadhaka pitta*, which governs the emotions and their
effect on the heart.

Silencer Tablet combines six herbs traditionally used across those three areas — adaptogens,
nervine tonics and one classical sedative herb — in a single coated tablet intended for use at
bedtime.

## The herbs, and why they are here

**Jatamansi (Nardostachys jatamansi)** — the rhizome yields jatamansone, and the herb is used in
ayurveda to relieve nervousness and irritability. It is traditionally described as working
synergistically with Ashwagandha and Brahmi, which is how it is used here.

**Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera)** — the best known Indian adaptogen. It is a *vata*
suppressant used to nourish the nervous system, support the body's resilience to stress and
support normal immune function.

**Mandukparni (Centella asiatica)** — known in India as a brain food. It is unusual in being
stimulating to mental clarity while remaining calming to an overactive nervous system.

**Brahmi (Bacopa monnieri)** — the principal *rasayana* for the nervous system, associated with
memory, concentration, tranquillity and cerebral circulation.

**Shankhpushpi (Evolvulus alsinoides)** — a classical brain tonic, used to support memory and
intellect.

**Khurasani Ajwain (Hyoscyamus niger)** — a traditional sedative and antispasmodic seed,
included in a small quantity to support the settling action of the formulation.
