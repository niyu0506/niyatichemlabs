## What it is

Soy Protein Isolate 90% is the most refined form of soy protein — the carbohydrate and fat
fractions have been removed, leaving a powder that is at least 90% protein on a dry basis. This
grade is **cold water soluble**, which matters in practice: it disperses without heating, so it
can go into a ready-to-mix sachet or a bottled beverage without a hot processing step.

It is unflavoured and unsweetened, which leaves the flavour system entirely in your hands.

## Functional properties

Beyond the protein number, this grade was selected for how it behaves in a formulation:

- **High affinity for water absorption and binding** — useful in bars, extruded products and
  anywhere texture depends on moisture control.
- **Excellent dispersion and fat emulsification** — it holds an emulsion rather than separating.
- **High dispersibility, low viscosity** — it mixes into a beverage without turning it into a gel,
  which is the usual complaint with cheaper protein grades.
- **Good antioxidant properties**, contributing to the stability of the finished product.
- **Clean flavour, functionality and mouthfeel**, so it does not fight the flavour system.

## Where it is used

Sports nutrition powders and RTD beverages · weight-management products · high-protein
fortification of foods · general health and wellness formats · as a dairy alternative in
plant-based beverages.
