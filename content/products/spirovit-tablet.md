## About this formulation

Spirovit Tablet is a single-ingredient product. Each coated tablet carries 500 mg of spirulina
powder, with excipients and a hydroxypropyl methylcellulose coating making up the rest of the
tablet. There is no herbal blend to balance here — the formulation exists to deliver a
meaningful daily quantity of spirulina in a form that is stable, easy to dose and simple to
label.

Spirulina is a blue-green algae that grows in both fresh and saline water. It is one of the most
nutrient-dense foods available in dried form, which is why it is used as a general nutritional
supplement rather than for a single indication.

## What spirulina supplies

**Vitamins and minerals** — spirulina is a recognised plant source of vitamins A, K1, K2 and
B12, and carries useful quantities of iron, manganese and chromium. This profile is the main
reason it is formulated as a daily vitamin support tablet.

**Phycocyanin** — the blue pigment that gives the algae its colour is its principal active
compound, and is associated with antioxidant and anti-inflammatory activity.

**Carotenoids and GLA** — spirulina also supplies carotenoid pigments, gamma-linolenic acid and
superoxide dismutase, a group of phytonutrients linked in traditional use to cellular protection.

**Protein and trace nutrients** — the algae is roughly two-thirds protein by dry weight, which
supports its long use in nutritional and convalescent contexts.

Spirulina has been studied in connection with allergic rhinitis symptoms, endurance and muscle
strength, and the maintenance of normal glucose and cholesterol levels. It is best positioned as
a nutritional supplement taken alongside, not in place of, a varied diet.
