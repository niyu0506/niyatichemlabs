## About this formulation

The thyroid is a small endocrine gland with a wide brief. Through the hormones it produces it
influences the rate of metabolism, protein synthesis and the body's sensitivity to other
hormones. When output drifts above or below its normal range, the effects are felt broadly —
unexplained gain or loss of weight, lethargy, changes in pulse rate and unsettled mood.

Thyropan Tablet is built on the ayurvedic reading of this as largely a *kapha* disturbance. It
combines single herbs with two classical *guggul* preparations in a 475 mg coated tablet, and is
intended as nutritional support alongside, not in place of, treatment prescribed by a physician.

## The herbs, and why they are here

**Apamarg (Achyranthes aspera)** — the largest single component. It is used in ayurveda to
balance *kapha* and *vata*, and is the classical herb for *Bhasmaka Roga*, a condition marked by
disordered appetite and metabolism.

**Kachnar Guggul** — a classical formula used where *kapha* has accumulated as growths or
swellings, including in thyroid and PCOS contexts. It is valued for its detoxifying and
*kapha*-reducing action.

**Yograj Guggul** — used to help clear toxins and to support circulation to bone, muscle and
nerve tissue, and so the general strength of the musculoskeletal and nervous systems.

**Peepalachal (Ficus religiosa)** — the bark of the peepal tree, one of the most widely used
materials in traditional Indian medicine, applied across metabolic, respiratory and digestive
indications.

**Aak (Calotropis procera)** — traditionally described as anti-inflammatory and cleansing, and
used as a digestive stimulant to keep absorption working smoothly.

**Shuddha Bhilamo (Semecarpus anacardium)** — purified marking nut, used in ayurveda as an
appetiser and digestive stimulant, and closely associated with *kapha* disorders.

**Akhrot (Juglans regia)** — walnut, a source of healthy fats traditionally used to support
normal cholesterol levels and healthy weight.
