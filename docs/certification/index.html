Certified, and happy to prove it

Our units at Vasai and Navsari hold GMP, WHO-GMP and ISO certification, and current copies of every certificate are available to buyers on request.

Note for review: the three certificate panels above are placeholders. The real scans go in once they are issued in the company’s own name — replace the files in static/img/cert/ and update the image: lines in this page’s settings block.

Certification is the result of a routine, not a substitute for one. Every consignment of raw material is analysed on arrival — identity, purity and, where relevant, assay of the marker compound — before it is accepted into stores. Material that fails specification is rejected rather than reworked.

Testing is done in our own laboratory on site, so results come back in hours rather than weeks and a doubtful batch can be held before it reaches production. In-process checks cover blend uniformity, weight, hardness and disintegration as appropriate to the dosage form. Finished batches are tested again and released only against a signed specification, and retained samples are held so that a question raised later can be answered against the material actually shipped.

Documentation you can ask for

A certificate of analysis accompanies every consignment as a matter of course. Beyond that we can supply batch manufacturing records, stability data, heavy-metal and microbial test reports, and the export paperwork your market requires. If your quality team or your regulator needs a particular document, ask for it — in most cases it is already on file.

GMP Certificate

Good Manufacturing Practice certification confirms that our premises, equipment, processes and record-keeping meet the standard required for consistent manufacture. For a buyer, it is the baseline evidence that the batch made last month and the batch made next month are the same product.

WHO-GMP Certificate

WHO-GMP applies the World Health Organization's schedule to our facility and our quality systems. It is the certification most often asked for by importers and regulatory authorities outside India, and it is what allows our material to be accepted in a number of export markets.

ISO Certificate

ISO certification covers the management system behind the manufacturing — how procedures are documented, how deviations are handled, and how customer feedback is acted on. It is the difference between quality that depends on individuals and quality that is built into the process.

In-house quality control

Raw materials are tested on arrival and finished batches are tested again before release. Certificates of analysis are issued with every consignment.

Documentation on request

Batch records, stability data, heavy-metal and microbial reports, and export documentation are available to buyers on request.
