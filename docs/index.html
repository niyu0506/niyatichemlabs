About Niyati Chem Labs

An Indian manufacturer with a global footprint

We are a Mumbai-based company with manufacturing units at Vasai, Maharashtra and Navsari, Gujarat — known for transparent dealings and for delivering on time. Our facilities are outfitted with modern equipment for product development, in-house testing, storage, packaging, labelling and dispatch.
